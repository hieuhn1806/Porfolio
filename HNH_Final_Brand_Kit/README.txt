# HNH — Personal Identity Final Brand Kit

This is a clean vector redraw of the selected mark. The original three-element construction and cyan/blue, deep-purple, neon-pink color transitions are preserved. All font outlines are included in SVG: no font download is required.

## Recommended use
- Portfolio header: HNH_Primary_Icon.svg, 28–40 px tall. Use icon only when space is limited.
- Hero, resume or social banners: HNH_Primary_Logo.svg.
- Dark backgrounds: HNH_Dark_Mode_Logo.svg and HNH_Dark_Mode_Icon.svg (lighter adjusted gradients for legibility).
- Single-color printing and embossing: HNH_Black_Logo.svg and HNH_White_Logo.svg.
- Favicon: HNH_Favicon_512.png, downscaled as necessary.

## Palette
- Cyan #16C9F6
- Electric Blue #0D6FF0
- Midnight #11102F
- Violet #5717CC
- Neon Orchid #EF3AEE
- Wordmark dark #10182A
- Dark background #0C1020

## Visual usage
- Preserve proportions and clear space. Do not stretch or recolor each letter randomly.
- For small sizes 32–48 px, use the symbol only. At very small sizes, a monochrome variant may reproduce more reliably.
- Gradient artwork prints differently in CMYK. Verify colors with printer.
- Digital brand artwork only; no trademark availability check has been performed.
