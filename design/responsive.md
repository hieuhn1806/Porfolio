# Responsive
Verify at 360px, 768px and 1440px plus any project-specific POS/hardware viewport.
At mobile: readable type, touch target comfort, clear main action, no accidental horizontal overflow.
At tablet: preserve orientation/task priority; reflow navigation and forms intentionally.
At desktop: tune max-width and data density, avoid unnecessarily huge gaps and full-width text.
For tables choose columns, row detail, controlled horizontal scroll or mobile cards deliberately.
Do not shrink a desktop dashboard until illegible. Provide actual screenshots and scroll evidence.
